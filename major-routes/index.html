Road Safety

Our South India Highway Transit Standards

Navigating South India's bustling national expressways demands professional discipline, route foresight, and constant mechanical vigilance. At CK Oneway Taxi, we uphold the highest road safety protocols:

  • Regulated Speed Governors: All commercial cabs are fitted with electronic speed limiters set according to state highway authority regulations, avoiding rash acceleration or unsafe overtaking.
  • Night Driving Fatigue Management: On trips scheduled between 10:00 PM and 6:00 AM, drivers are required to take scheduled rest pauses at well-lit fuel stations to ensure alert driving.
  • Tire & Brake Pre-Trip Inspections: Drivers verify tire tread depths, spare tire inflation, brake fluid levels, and windshield washer fluids before starting any route exceeding 200 km.
